What is a decision-support system?

A decision-support system is software that turns a hard operational choice — what to schedule, how to allocate a limited resource, which plan to choose among thousands of combinations — into a data-driven recommendation, using mathematical optimization instead of spreadsheets or guesswork.

They show up anywhere a business faces a choice too large for a person to work through by hand: which bags go on which flights, how many cashiers to schedule per store, which staffing plan satisfies every constraint. The system doesn't replace the decision-maker — it narrows thousands of possible answers down to the best one, in seconds.

That's the category of software I build: not a one-off analysis, but a system a business runs on every day.
